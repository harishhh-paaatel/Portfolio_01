[ 02 ] About / Story

Polished developer by default.
Creative technologist underneath.

I design and engineer digital products where performance, motion and AI meet — from pixel-perfect interfaces to the systems that power them.

My work sits between engineering and design. I care about how a product feels in the hand: the weight of an interaction, the timing of a transition, the moment a visitor discovers something they didn't expect. Behind that sits clean, typed, well-tested code that scales.

Lately I've been exploring how large language models can make interfaces more personal and more useful — building AI features that feel like part of the product, not a bolt-on chat box.
